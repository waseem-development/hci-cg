Admissions Fall 2026

Clear eligibility rules, transparent merit calculation, and need-based scholarships for BS, MPhil, MS, and PhD applicants.

edit_document Start Online Application payments View Fee Structure

How to Apply in Four Steps

Step 1

Verify Eligibility

Confirm minimum Intermediate (FA/FSc) or Bachelor's marks for your chosen department.

Step 2

Complete Portal Form

Upload CNIC/B-Form, domicile certificate, and attested academic transcripts.

Step 3

Entry Test & Merit

Appear in the departmental test or submit valid GAT/LAT scores where required.

Step 4

Challan & Enrollment

Deposit the semester fee challan at designated bank branches to confirm your seat.
