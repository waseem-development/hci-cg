About the University of Balochistan

Over five decades of public higher education, scientific inquiry, and civic leadership in Quetta and across Balochistan.

Our Heritage Since 1970

Established by ordinance in October 1970, the University of Balochistan began with three departments—Physics, Chemistry, and Geology—to meet the educational aspirations of Pakistan's largest province.

Today, our Sariab Road main campus and regional sub-campuses in Mastung, Pishin, and Kharan bring degree-level instruction, accredited laboratories, and library resources closer to communities across the province.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide equitable, affordable, and research-driven higher education that empowers students from all backgrounds to contribute to regional sustainability, public service, and national progress.

Our Vision

To stand as a nationally respected centre of academic excellence in arid-zone sciences, mineralogy, social inquiry, and Balochistan's rich linguistic and cultural heritage.
